-------------------------------------------------------
The analysis identifies Gaussian as the best overall filtering technique among the noise-specific best-performing filters, based on the average composite score.
The project evaluates image restoration under multiple noise conditions using PSNR, SSIM, MAE, edge preservation, runtime analysis, object detection, and shape descriptors.
The results provide a quantitative basis for selecting an appropriate filtering technique for noisy image data.
